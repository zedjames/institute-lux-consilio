Papers I–III

Constituting the quantum state

PaperI

Scientific question

What survives the completed matter-current constraint?

Closed Matter-Current Constraints and Gaussian Physical Moduli

Every nonzero exact joint current-zero state has unbounded particle-number support. The same closed kernel contains an injective three-parameter family of normalized Gaussian states.

Ψ ∈ K, Ψ ≠ 0 ⇒ ∀B ∃n: N(n) > B, Ψ(n) ≠ 0

Established boundary

No nonzero bounded-particle state satisfies the exact joint constraint. The finite-regulator result concerns the completed kernel, rather than removal of the regulator.

Mathematical inheritance: The closed-current Gaussian family and its all-orders pair recurrence become the input for Paper II.

Next inquiry: Can a finite mathematical object generate and identify the entire completed state?

PaperII

Scientific question

How is an infinite physical state generated by finite pair data?

Pair-Generated Completion of Gaussian Physical States

The symmetric pair kernel is the Cayley transform of the positive precision. A closed quadratic creator reconstructs every even Fock sector; the normalized two-particle seed identifies the Gaussian modulus.

Kₐ = (I − Gₐ)(I + Gₐ)⁻¹; Ξₐ = Vₐ⁻¹ P₂Ψₐ = Aₐ†Ω

Established boundary

Every nonzero finite pair truncation lies outside the exact joint current kernel, even though the sequence converges in norm. Finite identification and exact current physicality are distinct.

Mathematical inheritance: The faithful seed and the completed pair carrier provide the objects whose representation is qualified in Paper III.

Next inquiry: What physical and representation-theoretic meaning can be assigned to the generated pair?

PaperIII

Scientific question

When does a Fock representation acquire particle meaning?

From Pair-Generated Fock Structure to Physical Particle Semantics

The generated pair has a completed Bose/Fock unitary and transported Poincaré action. The sixty-coordinate bank has nonzero analytic representatives with constrained transport.

U_BF: H_Bose ≃ᵤ F⁽²⁾; E_kin ≠ E_null

Established boundary

The native kinetic and null asymptotic translation generators disagree. The tested full-Gaussian asymptotic junction is empty; this is a route-specific obstruction, not an exclusion of all later particle realizations.

Mathematical inheritance: The pair geometry and its analytically qualified carrier motivate the response investigation of Paper IV.

Next inquiry: Which physical information survives variational and radiative response maps?

Papers IV–VI

Testing the representation

PaperIV

Scientific question

What information survives gravitational response?

Gaussian Pair Geometry and Physical Radiative Response

The Gaussian covariance retains three moduli. The realized connection-quadratic Taylor density has zero variational stress. A separate selected hypercharge radiative branch retains one scale before normalization makes the repeated-source Bose pair scale independent.

Σₐ = Σᵦ ⇔ a = b; T⁽²⁾ = 0; Jₐʳᵃᵈ = [2/(1+a_Y)] J₀ʳᵃᵈ

Established boundary

The nonzero radiative branch uses an action residual and a selected profile. A map from the smooth variational stress to that completed metric source is open; the two branches are distinct.

Mathematical inheritance: The qualified radiative pair supplies an input in the ambient physical two-particle space used in Paper V.

Next inquiry: How does the physical celestial kernel organize the radiative two-particle space?

PaperV

Scientific question

What changes when celestial shadow reflection acts on a pair?

Source-Aware Weyl-Reduced Celestial Representation

The kernel-derived Weyl reflection acts jointly on the output label and source spectrum. Its fixed joint Hilbert space has a fundamental-domain reduction. A normalized spin-one transition gives a nonunit Gram factor on a positive-measure spectral set.

(J,ν; ω,k) ↦ (−J,−ν; ω+ν/π,k+2J); T₁,₀ = 1 − 4 log 2

Established boundary

Under the stated homogeneous/Fourier contact normalization, the tested fixed-source fiberwise isometry fails. The joint scalar reduction is distinct from the physical angular transform; the result does not exclude every change of source trivialization or every other normalization.

Mathematical inheritance: The source-aware kernel geometry and its analytic distinction are retained in Paper VI.

Next inquiry: Can the physical transform identify its source, and is it bounded in the original source norm?

PaperVI

Scientific question

Can a faithful representation fail to be bounded?

The Physical Celestial Clebsch–Gordan Transform of a Radiative Bose Pair

The intrinsic cross-ratio shape transform and full signed continuation are injective on the physical core-generated source range for every external-label pair. At selected opposite-helicity labels a bounded-energy sequence has divergent retained-output norm.

ker T_shape = ker T_full = {0}; ‖ψⱼ‖² ≤ 500π², ‖C_direct ψⱼ‖ → ∞

Established boundary

The original uniform Hilbert completion fails; at this stage retained-sector injectivity and closability remain open. The intrinsic and retained transforms must be distinguished.

Mathematical inheritance: Paper VII works with the literal coefficient on a dense admissible physical domain instead of assuming an everywhere-defined bounded map.

Next inquiry: Does exact source identification control gravitational interactions continuously?

Papers VII–IX

Interactions and physical geometry

PaperVII

Scientific question

Does celestial faithfulness guarantee stable dynamics?

Dynamical Ill-Conditioning of a Faithful Celestial Representation

The original retained coefficient is injective on its dense physical domain. A nonzero complete-action cubic descends uniquely to its exact image, but its Riesz vector lies outside the genuine partial adjoint range.

L₃(Ψⱼ)=1, ‖T_ret Ψⱼ‖→0; v_cub ∉ Ran T_ret*

Established boundary

A fixed nonzero physical response can correspond to retained outputs tending to zero. The source observable stays continuous in its physical Hilbert norm. The result is a finite-regulator interaction obstruction, not a continuum scattering amplitude.

Mathematical inheritance: The adjoint-range criterion and original cubic observable determine the first topology refinement in Paper VIII.

Next inquiry: What additional topology is necessary when fourth-order interactions are included?

Sections 4, 7–9. Version 2.0. Source edition DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23545548 · Current ILC publication record →

PaperVIII

Scientific question

Which topology supports the complete interaction family?

Interaction Topologies of Celestial Radiative States

The retained operator is densely defined, injective, unbounded and closable. On its exact image, cubic and fixed-action-leg quartic families require successive strict locally convex refinements. The quartic separation uses a physical seven-row augmented minor.

τ_ret ⊊ τ₃ ⊊ τ₃,₄; v₄ ∉ Ran T_ret* + span{v₃(θ)}

Established boundary

Continuity for the full admissible cubic span is insufficient for the selected quartic. The hierarchy is for the exact retained image and the declared action families; alternative source-recovering topologies are a separate comparison.

Mathematical inheritance: The retained physical carrier and complete-action interaction structures become the comparison basis for Paper IX.

Next inquiry: Which linear metric observation spaces can realize the nonlinear response of the action?

Source and edition

Theorems 3.1–3.2, 4.1–4.3; Section 5. Version 1.0. Source edition DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23651438 · Current ILC publication record →

PaperIX

Scientific question

What additional metric information does nonlinear gravitational response require?

From Interaction Topology to Nonlinear Gravitational Geometry

Six boundary controls determine an analytic response of an 810-dimensional Lorentz-reduced interior system, observed in 384 spatial components. Finite-packet quadratic and cubic quotient ranks are 21 and 56. A specified full coherent observation has radiative image equal to the sitewise trace kernel and exact second-order local linear closure.

Finite: 128 → 134 → 155 → 211; coherent: 320 → 326 → 347 → 347

Established boundary

The two observers have distinct linear images and cubic requirements. The quadratic obstruction persists under the stated common observation. Six nonlinear trace coordinates recover the local response; the 347-dimensional coherent linear space is not a count of propagating modes. These results concern the declared finite-regulator model.

Mathematical inheritance: The action, response derivatives, physical comparison spaces and reconstruction certificates provide a basis for further source-qualified geometric inquiry.

Next inquiry: How do higher-order directions, other observation maps and physically admissible evolution interact? These are continuing questions, not results of Paper IX.

Source and edition

Theorem 1.1; Sections 4.5, 5.4; Theorem 5.22; Appendix B. Version 1.0. Source edition DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24540001 · Current ILC publication record →
