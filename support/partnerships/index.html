Institutional inquiries welcome · Collaboration by arrangement

Support the capacity for discovery.

Concordia is being developed as scientific infrastructure for cumulative mathematical research and governed human–AI inquiry. Institutions can help broaden this capability through research participation, technical expertise, computational resources, independent verification and appropriately structured philanthropic support.

A partnership begins with a scientific or infrastructural objective. The parties identify the relevant source materials, responsibilities, anticipated outcomes and appropriate evidence of progress.

Participation opportunities

Where institutions can contribute.

Universities and research institutes

Joint investigations, specialist examination, formal mathematical collaboration and qualified researcher participation.

Scientific and philanthropic foundations

Research grants, infrastructure development, independent verification and the future participation of researchers.

AI research organizations

Source-grounded mathematical reasoning, long-horizon research, scientific evidence qualification and governed human–AI collaboration.

Computational infrastructure partners

Computing credits, model access, storage, reproducible environments and formal verification resources.

Measurement and experimental institutions

Observation theory, measurement sufficiency, physical modeling and independent empirical investigation.

Educational organizations

Inquiry-driven mathematical education, scholarly mentorship and future fellowship or graduate research opportunities.

Institutional principles

Meaningful participation, responsibly arranged.

Define a shared objective

Identify a scientific question or infrastructure need, the relevant research program, and the type of contribution.

Qualify scope and responsibilities

Agree upon scientific methods, evidence requirements, permitted access, security, publication, attribution, data and intellectual property arrangements as appropriate.

Evaluate scientific value

Assess results at their actual mathematical or empirical scope. Any ongoing relationship remains subject to institutional review and documented responsibilities.
