The generative power of scientific knowledge

The value of a discovery extends into everything it subsequently makes possible.

Scientific discoveries establish new possibilities for inquiry. A theorem can enable another theorem, an established obstruction can redirect a research program, and a physical relationship can inform a new method of observation.

ILC develops foundational research within Concordia, an intentionally interdependent mathematical environment. The objective is to preserve the capacity through which scientific knowledge can continue to participate in discovery across disciplines and generations.

Philanthropic support advances that objective and the development of opportunities for researchers to pursue their own questions.
