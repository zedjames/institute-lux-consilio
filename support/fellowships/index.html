Researcher-directed scientific inquiry

The future Concordia Scientific Discovery Fellowship is intended to enable researchers to pursue original scientific questions within an interconnected mathematical environment. The intellectual direction belongs to the fellow. Concordia’s formal constructions, research histories, counterexamples and qualified dependencies would provide resources for continuing inquiry.

The proposed program will explore how researchers can inherit existing scientific capacity, develop their mathematical expertise and contribute new, appropriately qualified discoveries. Launch, participation arrangements, eligibility, duration and funding depend on institutional resources, program design and Runtime qualification.

Alpha Testing · Closed beta targeted for Q2 2027. Timing and invitations depend on continuing testing and qualification.

Who the program is intended to serve

A breadth of scientific questions

The intended audience includes researchers in mathematics, physics, computational science, biological organization, engineering and measurement science, including early-career researchers and qualified graduate students. Final eligibility, selection and support arrangements will be published only when approved.

Researcher-directed inquiry

Begin with a question the researcher wishes to investigate; relate it to the existing scientific record.

Qualified Concordia access

Use source-identified mathematical and scientific material within the capabilities and access scope actually authorized.

Research computing

Develop appropriate AI, verification and computational support in accordance with the inquiry and available resources.

Mathematical development

Examine hypotheses and dependencies; construct proofs, counterexamples and source-qualified relationships.

Scientific collaboration

Develop opportunities for specialist feedback, learning and independent examination.

Scientific contribution

Document results, negative findings and open obligations; propose qualified contributions for review.

Proposed research progression

From a question to a qualified contribution

01

Define the question

Agree on a feasible scientific scope and the source materials needed to begin.

02

Inherit and examine

Reconstruct representative results or examine their evidence and established boundaries.

03

Develop the inquiry

Investigate a construction, extension, obstruction or qualified cross-program relationship.

04

Review and retain

Submit results and evidence for appropriate review. Record what is established, what remains open and what may be reusable.

The proposed pathway begins with a scientific question or contribution submitted through the research mailbox. A prospective arrangement would identify provenance, authorship, access, publication and intellectual property conditions before source work begins. A result would be considered for admission only after its mathematical scope, dependencies, evidence and applicable permissions are examined. This description does not establish automatic intake or admission.
