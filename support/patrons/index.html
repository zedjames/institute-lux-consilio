Support the capacity for discovery.

The opportunity for founding scientific patrons is to participate in the formative development of a scientific environment whose value extends through future discoveries. Concordia preserves and connects mathematical constructions, scientific results, established obstructions and research histories so they can participate in subsequent inquiry.

Individuals and family foundations are invited to discuss philanthropic support for this work. Sustained support can help establish the continuity required for research infrastructure, independent examination and qualified participation across disciplines.

Support the Institute’s charitable scientific and educational work, including the continuity of foundational research, mathematical infrastructure and scholarly communication.

Scientific infrastructure

Independent examination

Help make specialist review, mathematical reconstruction and qualified scholarly engagement possible.

Future researcher participation

Discuss support for onboarding, computational resources and the development of research fellowships as the necessary program arrangements are established.

Multi-year patronage

Explore a sustained philanthropic commitment aligned with the Institute’s actual development milestones, resources and charitable mission.

Project-specific support

Discuss a defined scientific or infrastructure objective with an agreed scope, appropriate oversight and reporting arrangements.

Institutional principles

Meaningful participation, responsibly arranged.

Scientific independence

Scientific conclusions remain governed by mathematical and empirical evidence. A contribution creates no scientific authority, ownership of research results or entitlement to restricted sources.

Recognition and privacy

Recognition, when appropriate and agreed, may acknowledge philanthropic participation. Donor names are used only with permission; reporting and correspondence arrangements are discussed before acceptance.

Responsible stewardship

Gift purpose, restrictions, reporting and any relevant conflicts are evaluated through appropriate institutional arrangements. ILC’s charitable activities are distinct from Fieldflux Biosystems’ commercial activities.
