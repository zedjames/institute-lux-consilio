Governance & research integrity

Protect the conditions that let discovery happen.

The institute's governance is built to support scientific independence, clear provenance, long-term continuity, and responsible movement between research, technology, and clinical work.

Follow the evidence.

Positive, negative, null, ambiguous, and adverse results all contribute information. The record matters because the next question often appears where an expectation fails.

Keep provenance visible.

Research outputs carry their scientific and institutional origin. Material intercompany research, services, intellectual-property, and technology-transfer transactions receive their own records and approvals.

Let discoveries travel responsibly.

Technology transfer agreements preserve scientific use, publication, education, verification, and continued research while commercial partners take on the work of product development.
