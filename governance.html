The institute

Governance & research integrity

A durable research culture depends on scientific independence, clear provenance, and responsible stewardship.

Scientific integrity

Our research records assumptions, methods, negative results, uncertainties, and the precise scope of established claims.

Publication

ILC supports scholarly writing and scientific communication, with appropriate protections for patents, confidentiality, and third-party obligations.

Institutional stewardship

ILC maintains its own governance, accounting, and scientific decision-making. Relationships with separately incorporated organizations, including Fieldflux Biosystems, are evaluated according to their respective institutional purposes, with appropriate documentation and conflict-of-interest review for any related-party arrangements.

Scientific continuity

Research programs retain the provenance of their publications, authors, affiliations, and supporting records. Scientific-use, publication, licensing, and other intellectual-property rights are governed by the applicable ownership and agreements; new arrangements are documented before they are represented as established.

Legal identity

Nonprofit public charity

Institute Lux Consilio is a Delaware nonprofit non-stock corporation, recognized as tax-exempt under §501(c)(3) and classified under §509(a)(2).
