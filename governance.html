Governance & research integrity

Scientific independence is part of the structure.

ILC's governance and intercompany arrangements are designed to preserve scientific integrity, institutional continuity, and clear boundaries between research, commercial translation, and clinical work.

Scientific integrity

Research conclusions are expected to follow the scientific record. Negative, null, ambiguous, and adverse results are part of that record and are not treated as failures merely because they are commercially inconvenient.

Publication

ILC retains the ability to publish scientific work. Limited prepublication review may protect confidential information, patent filing, legal restrictions, and factual accuracy, but commercial interests do not determine scientific conclusions.

Conflicts and related-party transactions

ILC and organizations it controls or collaborates with remain separate legal entities. Material intercompany research, services, intellectual-property, and technology-transfer transactions are separately documented with their purpose, resources, approvals, and economic terms.

Technology transfer

Commercial translation of ILC-owned technology is handled through specific agreements. The institute's default is to retain scientific, scholarly, educational, verification, preservation, and publication rights even when commercial rights are licensed.

Institutional control

ILC holds a protected institutional-control interest in Fieldflux Biosystems. That structure is designed to preserve long-term mission alignment while FFB remains a separate taxable commercial corporation responsible for its own operations, assets, liabilities, financing, and products.

Nonprofit status

Institute Lux Consilio is a Delaware nonprofit non-stock corporation and a tax-exempt public charity under §501(c)(3), classified under §509(a)(2).
