Governance & research integrity

Integrity gives curiosity room to travel.

ILC's governance supports scientific independence, clear provenance, long-term research continuity, and transparent relationships among research, technology translation, and clinical work.

Follow the evidence.

Research conclusions grow from the scientific record. Positive, negative, null, ambiguous, and adverse results all contribute information and help determine the next question.

Publish the science.

ILC's publication framework supports scholarly communication while giving appropriate time for patent filing, factual review, confidentiality, and legal obligations. Scientific conclusions remain grounded in the research itself.

Keep provenance visible.

Material intercompany research, services, intellectual-property, and technology-transfer transactions receive their own records, approvals, purposes, and economic terms. Scientific work also carries its originating capacity so future readers can understand where it came from and how it developed.

Let discoveries travel responsibly.

Commercial translation of ILC-owned technology uses specific agreements. ILC's retained scientific rights support continued research, education, verification, preservation, publication, and future discovery.

Build for the long horizon.

ILC holds a protected institutional-control interest in Fieldflux Biosystems, supporting long-term mission alignment while Fieldflux carries the responsibilities of commercial development.

Public charity

Institute Lux Consilio is a Delaware nonprofit non-stock corporation and a tax-exempt public charity under §501(c)(3), classified under §509(a)(2).
