Relational Qualities and the Constitution of Physical Response

Bogoliubov dynamics, radiative Bose composition, and gravitational response

Zed James · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24600824 ↗ · v1.0

Scientific abstract and context

Relational information and physical response

Paper I investigates how relational qualities develop through physical transformation and determine subsequent responses. In a Schwarzschild scalar Fock sector, anomalous correlations distinguish later occupations and the quadratic moments obey exact two-time dynamics with a conserved Gram determinant. In the radiative Bose sector, a finite gravitational observation has an infinite-dimensional canonical full-history quotient, while finite packet spaces admit stable amplitude reconstruction. A source-matched Einstein–scalar family exhibits invariant curvature separation and fourth-order physical scalar response in the matched conformal clock.

The scalar moment law is valid on the specified free-Hamiltonian domain without a Gaussian-state restriction. The radiative result concerns the complete free mixed-load response, with complex-linear amplitude tests on specified packet spaces. The matched Einstein–scalar comparison uses fixed source strengths on each branch.
