Present-State Resolution and Lineage History in Early Mouse Embryogenesis

Matched-control lineage analysis, fully inductive molecular prediction, and representation-aware history tests in E7.5 mouse embryos.

Zed James · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23125763 ↗

Scholarly record

Research summary

Across three E7.5 MELA embryos, the fully inductive molecular present predicts a disjoint contemporaneous molecular target beyond coarse annotations; matched controls and family-wise calibration leave the additional predictive contribution from the tested reconstructed-lineage summaries unestablished.

This catalog summary is provided for orientation. Please consult the complete manuscript for its original abstract and mathematical results.
