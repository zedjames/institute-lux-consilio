Gaussian Physical States & Celestial Representation

Tracking physical information through Gaussian, radiative, and celestial representations.

Guiding question

What exists, what is represented, what is identified, what is lost, and what remains reconstructible at each physical transition?

Scientific purpose

From Celestial Data to Physical Correspondence

Celestial scattering amplitudes can exhibit conformal transformation laws, operator-product structures, and Ward identities derived from the soft and collinear behavior of scattering processes. These relationships provide powerful mathematical information about the underlying theory.

But what establishes the physical and mathematical correspondence required to interpret such structures as the operators, interactions, and symmetries of an interacting celestial theory? Which conclusions follow from an established scattering construction, and which require additional assumptions about the proposed celestial realization?

The Gaussian Physical States and Celestial Representation program investigates these questions from the foundations of the physical construction. It develops the relevant states, operators, domains, symmetries, and representation geometry, then examines what information survives representation, which gravitational observables remain continuous, and how those structures behave under changes of regulator and physical realization.

The resulting papers trace a continuing investigation through Gaussian physical states, multiparticle structure, radiative representation, source faithfulness, Hilbert-space obstructions, and gravitational interaction. Each result determines what has been established within its mathematical setting, where additional structure is required, and which further questions have become accessible.

The program's objective is to develop the physical and mathematical foundations through which an interacting celestial correspondence can be investigated and tested. Its findings complement amplitude-based celestial holography while preserving the distinction between established scattering identities and the additional requirements of a physically realized celestial theory.

Program

Research trajectory

The sequence follows physical information through successive changes of representation. It begins with an exactly solvable Gaussian sector under a closed matter-current constraint, where nonzero current-physical states require an all-orders completion even though a finite two-particle seed can remain faithful to the Gaussian modulus.

The middle of the series separates representation-theoretic existence from physical particle semantics, then follows the same pair geometry into radiative response. Different response maps retain different parts of the original provenance, making information compression an explicit theorem of the construction.

Papers V and VI develop the source-aware celestial Clebsch–Gordan representation of the radiative Bose pair. Fixed-source Weyl isometry fails, but the intrinsic cross-ratio and full signed transforms are source-faithful at every physical external-label pair. Paper VI v2.0 settles the previously conditional retained Hilbert-completion question negatively: bounded-energy physical sources can have divergent retained output, and no common almost-everywhere decomposable bound holds in the declared norms.

Paper VII v2.0 uses the original normalized retained coefficient on a dense physical Bose-pair domain, without restoring the bounded completion disproved by Paper VI. For a finite-regulator cubic generated by ordered third variations of the complete action, it establishes exact source identification and an exact-range gravitational response, but proves that response is discontinuous in the retained-output Hilbert norm: the cubic Riesz vector lies outside the true partial-adjoint range. A response-normalized family has fixed nonzero cubic output and vanishing retained coefficient.

Paper VIII v1.0 proves that the original physical retained Clebsch–Gordan map is densely defined, injective, unbounded and closable, with a canonical closed source realization on a proper dense domain. On its exact retained image the complete-action cubic and fixed-action-leg quartic impose successive strict topological refinements, τret ⊊ τ3 ⊊ τ3,4. A true partial-adjoint and full-cubic-family obstruction, grounded in a seven-row physical action certificate, establishes the new quartic step. Regulator results distinguish compatible native null-face decay from occupied-to-null-face and static-transport obstructions; a source-recovering projective carrier offers a separate continuity framework. These claims do not construct scattering amplitudes or celestial OPEs.

Interaction Topologies of Celestial Radiative States

Cubic and quartic continuity on the physical retained image

Paper VIII classifies the physical retained celestial Clebsch–Gordan operator on its dense radiative Bose-pair domain as injective, unbounded and closable, with a canonical closed realization on a proper dense source domain. On the exact retained image it establishes two strict interaction-continuity refinements, τret ⊊ τ3 ⊊ τ3,4, for the complete-action gravitational cubic and fixed-action-leg quartic families. The quartic obstruction excludes a physical Riesz vector from the genuine partial-adjoint range plus the entire admissible cubic span, with a seven-row complete-action packet certificate and exact bounds on five polarization moments. Native regulator tests establish compatible null-face quartic decay on a specified scalar-vacuum branch, a nonzero occupied-to-null-face defect, and selected mesh and radius-child transport obstructions. A stronger source-recovering carrier offers a distinct continuous topology. No continuum scattering operator, universal interacting celestial OPE, or regulator-uniform nonlinear action equivalence is constructed.

Current frontier

What the public record hands forward.

Papers VI–VIII now distinguish three source-specific boundaries: the retained coefficient does not admit uniform bounded Hilbert completion in the stated norms; the complete-action cubic gravitational response fails continuity in the inherited retained-output topology; and the additional physical fixed-leg quartic requires a further strict refinement on the same exact retained image. Paper VIII establishes a canonical closed partial realization and regulator-conditional compatibility and obstruction tests, but it does not establish action-independent transport, a continuum scattering operator, or interacting celestial operator products. The next frontier is to construct scattering-compatible asymptotic domains, physical evolution and regulator-transferred complete actions before testing connected amplitudes, factorization, crossing, soft-charge relations, celestial OPEs and Ward identities.
