The Physical Celestial Clebsch–Gordan Transform of a Radiative Bose Pair

Intrinsic source faithfulness, direct-integral geometry, and the obstruction to uniform Hilbert completion

Zed James · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23509540 ↗

Scholarly record

Research summary

The normalized physical celestial Clebsch–Gordan kernel distinguishes intrinsic radiative Bose-pair sources: the cross-ratio shape transform and full signed principal-series continuation are injective for every physical external-label pair. An explicit bounded-energy opposite-helicity source sequence nevertheless has divergent retained-output norm, disproving the uniform source-Hilbert completion estimate and excluding a common decomposable almost-everywhere bound. The source-Weyl geometry survives, but the retained coefficient requires a closed linear-relation/quotient treatment; closability, a natural replacement operator domain, and retained-sector injectivity remain open. The construction is noninteracting and establishes no scattering amplitude.
