Ways of knowing

Every question asks for its own instrument.

Sometimes the instrument is an equation. Sometimes it is a proof assistant, a simulation, a biological dataset, a physical sensor, or a carefully chosen comparison.

01MathematicsStructure, relation, geometry, continuation, invariance, and the exact shape of a claim.
02Formal proofMachine-checkable definitions and theorems that make complex reasoning inspectable and reusable.
03ComputationExperiments in representation, calibration, reconstruction, simulation, and biological organization.
04ObservationMeasurement and evidence that connect an abstract structure to a physical or biological system.

A scientific habit

Ask what the method can reveal, and what kind of claim it makes possible.

Rigor is curiosity made durable.

Formal verification, sensitivity analysis, calibration, controls, reproducible computation, and careful provenance help us understand why a result holds and how far it travels.

Different forms of evidence illuminate different layers. A theorem establishes a mathematical proposition. A computation can expose a structure. An experiment can connect a model to the world. A measurement becomes scientifically useful when its provenance and calibration are understood.
