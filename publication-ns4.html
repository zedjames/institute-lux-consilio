Faithful Terminal Descent of Autonomous Histories in Periodic Navier–Stokes Flow

Strong original-field recovery, exact proper-past energy, and registered observations

Zed James · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23378029 ↗

Scholarly record

Research summary

Conditional on finite maximal lifespan of a smooth unforced periodic three-dimensional Navier–Stokes history, amplitude-normalized records yield a bounded nonzero suitable ancient core. On fixed compact physical–spectral queries, original velocity, unit-ball-gauged canonical pressure, localized Fourier observations, and signed nonlinear work recover their same-core counterparts. Strong proper-past gradient convergence yields exact viscous dissipation and local kinetic-energy equality; countably registered queries admit one strict refinement. The initial-to-ancient energy balance requires positive historical donor work at each fixed sufficiently large radius. These are necessary properties of hypothetical breakdown histories, without a blowup construction or global-regularity conclusion.
