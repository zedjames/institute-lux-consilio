The institute

Built to let science continue.

Institute Lux Consilio is an independent nonprofit research institute dedicated to foundational scientific, mathematical, and computational research.

Mission

ILC develops formal frameworks, conducts scientific inquiry, publishes primary research, maintains research infrastructure, and collaborates across disciplines on questions whose importance extends beyond any single commercial application.

A permanent home for foundational research.

Institution before project

Research programs can outlive a paper, grant, product cycle, company, or individual technical implementation. ILC is designed to hold that longer time horizon: to preserve the scientific question, its provenance, its methods, and the ability to continue the work.

Founder and principal scientist

ILC was founded by Zed James, who serves as the institute's principal scientist and current institutional leader. The institute is being structured so its scientific programs become durable institutional assets rather than remaining dependent on one person's continued participation.

Legal form

Institute Lux Consilio is a Delaware nonprofit non-stock corporation and a tax-exempt public charity under §501(c)(3), classified under §509(a)(2).

Institutional design

Research, translation, and clinical practice remain distinct.

Formal agreements allow knowledge and technology to move between scientific, commercial, and clinical domains without collapsing their responsibilities.
