Current research

Programs accumulate. Papers become their record.

ILC organizes research around durable questions. Each program gathers papers, research notes, formal artifacts, code, data, and subsequent work into a scholarly history that can grow for years.

Scientific continuity

Research can become technology without becoming subordinate to technology.

ILC is structured to preserve scientific inquiry independently of any single commercial program. Research may lead to patents, products, companies, or clinical applications while the institute retains the ability to continue the underlying scientific work, publish results, collaborate, verify, and pursue new directions.
