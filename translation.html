Scientific continuity

Discovery opens possibilities.

Institute Lux Consilio develops, studies, and stewards foundational scientific work across mathematics, physics, biology, and measurement science. Its scholarly record supports independent investigation, verification, and collaboration.

Fieldflux Biosystems is a separately incorporated, independently governed commercial company developing health-measurement software, sensing systems, and research instruments informed by related scientific work. The institutions have distinct missions and responsibilities, with opportunities for scientific exchange and responsible research translation.

Scientific records retain their original authorship, affiliations, publication provenance, and applicable intellectual-property ownership. Any sponsored research, licensing, or technology-transfer arrangements require their own authorization and agreements.
