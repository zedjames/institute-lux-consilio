Build the application

Selected discoveries can move into product engineering, hardware, software, regulatory development, manufacturing, financing, and commercialization.

Science continues to unfold.

ILC retains broad scientific-use rights in the existing Fieldflux technology estate so research, verification, writing, publication, preservation, and scientific collaboration can continue across future commercial developments.

New discoveries create new paths.

Independent future ILC research belongs to ILC under the institute's governing framework. Fieldflux receives a structured first commercial opportunity for new ILC technology related to its field, creating a clear path from discovery into application.

Clinical work brings another layer of responsibility.

Clinical collaborations use dedicated patient, data, protocol, consent, privacy, and regulatory structures suited to medicine and human-subject research.

One discovery can support many futures.

A scientific result can become a paper, a theorem library, a new experiment, a research collaboration, a patent, an instrument, or the starting point for another field of inquiry. The institute is built to keep those possibilities open and legible.
