Scientific continuity

Discovery opens possibilities.

Institute Lux Consilio advances foundational research across mathematics, physics, biology, and measurement science. Its publications, formal methods, and scientific programs provide a basis for continued investigation and exchange.

A current example of professional collaboration is ILC's scientific exchange with Fieldflux Biosystems. Work on the formal definition of prospective health and its observation requirements meets FFB's development of health-measurement software, sensors, and instruments. Questions about physical observables, calibration, and validation help connect the ongoing investigations.

Exchange and continuity

Ideas grow through use and investigation.

Scientific results can open new paths for instruments and experiments. Engineering and observation, in turn, introduce questions about what can be measured, how reliably, and under which conditions. ILC welcomes collaborations with academic, nonprofit, and commercial partners on these shared research questions.
