Inventory clarification · November 28, 2026

What the source-line inventory measures

The physical source-line count includes mathematical definitions, theorem statements, proof terms, comments, and generated formal certificate material. These different kinds of source serve distinct scientific functions. Counts of files, lines and lexical declarations describe the extent of the source corpus; they are not counts of independent scientific arguments or discoveries.

Concentration in the celestial interaction research program

A substantial portion of the corpus derives from the mathematical program opened by Paper VII, Dynamical Ill-Conditioning of a Faithful Celestial Representation. Its first live gravitational cubic response exposes a discontinuity in the inherited retained celestial topology. The subsequent Celestial Interaction Branch Atlas (CIBA-01) investigates interaction topology, adjoint-range geometry, physical action geometry, cubic and quartic response, continuum covariance and the prerequisites for scattering-related constructions. Its results inform Paper VIII, Interaction Topologies of Celestial Radiative States.

The largest historical increase in source lines occurred between September 24 and October 17. The October census already recorded 19,225 atlas files containing 13,436,215 lines (71.0% of the full corpus). At the November 28 revision, the atlas contains 20,713 Lean files and 13,588,570 lines (60.6% of the corpus). The figures are compatible with a growing corpus whose largest individual concentration of source remains this certificate-intensive program.

A structured finite certification of physical interaction calculations

The dominant family consists of 12,288 generated fourth-order certificate files containing 11,009,792 lines — 81.0% of the atlas and 49.1% of the entire November Lean corpus. This material represents one fixed, highly structured numerical certification, expressed in exact rational arithmetic. The calculation is partitioned into three faces, each with 256 rows and 16 column chunks per row. Each chunk contains 16 cells; each cell tabulates lower and upper rational bounds for 45 intermediate calculation nodes.

The generator generate_face_program_table.py supplies the rational enclosure tables, local arithmetic checks and exact sums for five output slots. Each chunk certificate checks its cell-node conditions and records the endpoint sums. The third-order certificate family likewise materializes exact rational-polynomial and connection tables for the ordered cubic calculation bank, with proofs binding those tables to the source expressions.

From finite certificates to a physical obstruction

01 · Arithmetic

Whole-cell enclosures

Rational interval data and checks certify the intermediate calculation on every partition cell, not merely at sampled points.

02 · Physical integration

Five moment bounds

CertifiedFaceProgram.lean establishes the passage from checked node and cell enclosures to bounds on the original physical moment integrals, under the stated partition and integrability conditions.

03 · Source-bound rank

A nonzero matrix minor

Subsequent modules use actual physical moment ratios and a normalized seven-by-seven augmented minor to establish the required nonvanishing on the declared physical bank.

04 · Interaction topology

The full-family obstruction

ActualFullFamilyObstruction.lean consumes the source-bound minor result. Under the stated physical-carrier and analytic assumptions, the selected quartic Riesz vector lies outside the full admissible cubic span modulo the retained adjoint. The joint cubic–quartic interaction topology is strictly finer than the full cubic topology.

This chain is the scientific purpose of the certificate family: finite arithmetic evidence is connected through formal soundness and source-binding arguments to a precise structural result about gravitational interaction continuity. It is mathematical certification of the specified interaction model, rather than observational astronomy data or millions of separate conceptual derivations.

Verification and scientific scope

The CIBA branch decision matrix records resolution of the original A–H first-decision scope. It reports targeted compilation and axiom audits, distinguishes numerical/containment/minor laws using the standard logical axioms from physical consequences retaining the frozen-input-relative Lean.ofReduceBool and Lean.trustCompiler dependencies, and preserves separate evidence boundaries for continuum scattering, interacting OPE, soft limits and Ward constructions. No broader scattering or all-orders result follows merely from the size of this certificate bank.
