Notes from the edge of the work.

A question appears. A distinction becomes visible. A negative result changes the path. A connection forms between fields. Research Notes preserve those moments while the work is still becoming.

The notes record emerging ideas, methodological discoveries, calibration findings, formal milestones, and conceptual bridges across the institute's programs.
