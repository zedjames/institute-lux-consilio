One continuing scientific architecture

Different systems. Interacting mathematical foundations.

ILC's research programs examine distinct physical and mathematical questions within a cumulative Formal World. Their results can inform later stages of the same investigation and, where explicit interfaces are established, new relationships between programs.

The Schwarzschild Quantum Gravity series shows how one physical question progresses through increasingly demanding operator, matter, action, gauge, geometric, and boundary conditions. Formal Scientific Infrastructure develops the reusable mathematical and evidentiary structures that support such work.

01 Schwarzschild Quantum Gravity · Formal research in development

Quantum Observation and Gravitational Information

Which quantum properties remain observationally distinguishable beyond the complete Einstein-source description?

Evidence boundary: The result concerns the constructed fixed-background, finite-bank Fock carrier and a proper spatial-circle test window. The associated observation is not a strictly local AQFT observable or physical black-hole detector: no causal commutator theorem, detector coupling, general density-state instrument, cross-geometry transport, post-measurement stress-domain preservation or self-consistent gravitational backreaction is established. The QGCO result is internal formal research, not a published paper.

Next: Determine quantitative distinguishability; prove when the complete instrument transports through matched Fock frames and physically constrained representations; then test domain-preserving conditional stress and Einstein–scalar closure.

02 Schwarzschild Quantum Gravity · Continuing scientific investigation

Physical Continuation and Representational Boundaries

What structure must persist for an original excitation to retain its identity as its representation changes?

Evidence boundary: A local classical continuation does not establish indefinite continuation, free-boundary stationarity in all variation classes, or transport of the full original quantum representation.

Next: Investigate local identity-preserving mode maps, justified quantum transport, and the distinct mathematical obligations of new boundary representations.

03 Kinetic Organization and Biological Function · Continuing scientific investigation

Regenerative Functional Maintenance

What dynamical constraints maintain organized transport, recovery and function through structural turnover?

Evidence boundary: Developing regenerative bounds belong to specified synthetic physical sources and mathematical preparation classes; they do not establish general biological aging, clinical outcomes, or an experimentally calibrated mechanism.

Next: Clarify attractor structure, causal kinetic lag, loss of regenerative capacity and the mathematical onset of non-maintainability.

04 Essence and Physical Constitution · Emerging initiative

Essence and Physical Constitution

When do the properties attributed to an object arise through its interactions, constraints and relationships?

Evidence boundary: This is a developing research initiative. Its proposed constitutive relationships are not established as universal physical laws or as a completed published research series.

Next: Specify elementary physical carriers, their interaction maps and observables, then test whether different descriptions define the same constitutive object.

05 CH — Celestial Holography and Scattering · Formal research in development

From Gravitational Action to Physical Scattering

What additional boundary, constraint and asymptotic structures turn a source-derived kinematic scattering calculation into a physical scattering system?

Evidence boundary: Paper IX proves source-specific nonlinear gravitational reconstruction and boundary geometry in a prescribed finite regulator; it does not establish a physical S-matrix. The separately developing P9-SA11 kinematic nonidentity result concerns sufficiently small positive real couplings, with the original unit-coupling claim open. The nonlinear future-frame W6 readback obstruction and the original released-boundary variation require an enlarged action-derived physical carrier, appropriate Euler/Cartan flux, and coupled Bose-pair/gauge/BRST preservation before physical or celestial scattering amplitudes can be inferred.

Next: Develop a source-derived phase space with physically justified boundary equations and Cartan flux, incorporating any necessary extra geometric degrees of freedom; prove invariant W6/CH24 Bose-pair and gauge/BRST constraints; distinguish the original unit-coupling kinematic question from physical scattering, then test on-shell channels and celestial transform eligibility.
