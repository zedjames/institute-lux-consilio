Research atlas

Programs, not isolated papers.

The primary unit of ILC research is the scientific program: a durable question that accumulates papers, proofs, calculations, computational experiments, notes, failures, and subsequent directions.

Reading the archive

Each program has a history.

A paper can establish a result. A program records why that result mattered, what it depended upon, what failed, what became possible next, and where the boundary of the claim sits. ILC's archive is designed to preserve that history rather than flatten research into a feed.

As the institute grows, program pages will also carry formal-verification status, computational supplements, data references, version history, and related research notes.
