Kinetic Organization and Biological Function

How organized movement and coupled physical dynamics produce specified biological functions.

Guiding question

How does mechanochemical transport generate selected function through shared resources, and which kinetic information survives the passage from individual processes to collective dynamics?

Program

Research trajectory

Paper I establishes a reversible momentum-bearing mechanochemical transporter under prescribed chemical and ionic boundaries. Its hybrid physical generator identifies information lost under coarse phase/energy observation and gives certified function-specific transport and caloric results.

Paper II couples active transport, passive ionic return, chemical regeneration, finite shared inventories and membrane charge. A full kinetic invariant analysis distinguishes closed reversible Gibbs equilibrium from maintained nonequilibrium circulation with positive entropy production and zero mean net charge drift; its selected charge-band functionals quantify persistence, exit and recovery.

Paper III moves to populations of momentum-bearing transporters interacting through common ionic and chemical resources and an inventory-derived capacitor. Finite-population generators converge on stated positive finite windows to a nonlinear measure-valued kinetic–chemical–electrical law, with conditional propagation of chaos and selected fluctuation laws preserving physical shared-event covariance.

Collective prediction remains function-specific. Equal-energy population preparations with identical concentrations, phase fractions and voltage can produce different transport currents, and the actual loaded-rate generator has no invariant finite polynomial-momentum dictionary. A maintained Boltzmann-template projection retains an explicit discarded-kinetic residual, giving a reproducible strict finite-time charge-decrease certificate for a specified matched preparation. The series remains based on declared synthetic mechanisms; qìjī and qìhuà provide historical motivation without a claimed microscopic identification.

Paper IV examines the long-time organization of the same full kinetic source. Exact stationary reduction and rigorous source-specific interval enclosures identify one maintained stationary law with sustained ionic/chemical circulation, nontrivial phase–mechanical correlations and an in-band capacitor charge. It distinguishes closed Gibbs equilibrium from maintained organization, proves full-kinetic local attraction, and establishes a compact long-time attracting set supporting positive invariant-mean currents and recurring charge-band occupation. The finite-population indefinite-survival probability vanishes for each fixed population while the stationary collective limit stays in-band over every fixed horizon. The research retains the interpretive distinction between 氣機 (qìjī, organized movement) and 氣化 (qìhuà, material transformation) without claiming a calibrated biological realization.

Paper V completes the global-selection analysis of this maintained kinetic source. The complete position–momentum–phase model is decomposed into shared-resource/bulk coordinates and kinetic shape; a fifteen-pair positive bulk comparison and uniform nonautonomous twelve-input kinetic response yield a strict four-current rational feedback certificate below 17/20. Every bounded complete attractor trajectory must therefore equal the previously certified maintained stationary state, giving the singleton global attractor A = {X*} and attraction of bounded admissible preparation classes. Stationary interacting-population empirical laws converge weakly to δX*, with selected charge, material-current, chemical-work and caloric observables. The population/time limits agree for the specified bounded continuous state observables, while uninterrupted finite-population charge-band survival retains different iterated limits. The selected state remains physically active, with positive circulation, regeneration and energetic turnover.

Methods

  • Invariant kinetic laws and nonequilibrium circulation for the one-transporter source
  • Nonlinear empirical-measure limits, kinetic martingales, conditional chaos and selected fluctuations
  • Observation-fiber nonclosure and residual-controlled finite-time collective predictions
  • Full kinetic stationary-response enclosures, semigroup stability, Hellinger dissipation, compact attractors and finite-population first-exit controls
  • Four-current global small-gain certificate from a fifteen-pair positive bulk comparison, source-specific nonautonomous kinetic hypocoercivity, and twelve-input finite-to-infinite response bounds
  • Singleton attractor reconstruction, invariant stationary interacting-population laws, and the differing limits for state versus uninterrupted path observables

Results concern a specified synthetic source rather than a calibrated protein or a general physical definition of qi.

Shared finite reservoirs, sustained nonequilibrium circulation, and selected charge-band persistence

Nonlinear population limits, kinetic observability, and collective functional prediction

A synthetic population of momentum-bearing transporters interacts through shared finite ionic and chemical reservoirs and an inventory-derived membrane capacitor. The exact diffusion–jump source yields a well-posed nonlinear kinetic–chemical–electrical population law, finite-time convergence under specified positive-resource preparations, conditional propagation of chaos and selected Gaussian fluctuations with shared-event covariations. Explicit equal-energy preparations demonstrate distinct currents despite matching concentrations, phase fractions and energy, and the loaded-current observable has no invariant finite polynomial-momentum generator dictionary. A residual-controlled maintained-source projection certifies 1.8932060449 < χ(0.05) < 1.9954528308 < χ(0) = 2 for a prescribed matched preparation; thermodynamic, entropy and charge-band results retain their declared conditions. Stationary Organization and Functional Persistence in Collective Kinetic Systems

Full kinetic stationary classification, local stability, and persistent physical function

Paper IV studies stationary organization and long-time function in the synthetic collective kinetic–chemical–electrical transporter source of Papers I–III. The complete position–momentum–phase kinetic response gives exactly one maintained positive stationary organization with ongoing pump, passive-return and regeneration circulation, charge within the prescribed [1, 3] band, phase–position correlation, and non-Gaussian momentum structure. The full kinetic–resource linearization and nonlinear estimates establish an explicit local attracting neighborhood, while a separate compact attracting set supports strictly positive invariant-mean circulation and quantitative long-time charge-band recurrence. Native finite-population event paths eventually exit the band with probability one for each fixed population, whereas the stationary collective limit remains in-band over every fixed finite horizon; the resulting population/time survival limits do not commute. The work does not establish global point selection or identify the compact attractor with the stationary state; these remain for Paper V.

Global Selection of Collective Kinetic Organization

Nonautonomous kinetic response, attractor uniqueness, and stationary population limits

Paper V establishes global selection of the unique maintained stationary organization of the original nonlinear four-phase kinetic–chemical–electrical transporter system. The full position–momentum–phase law is coupled to finite shared inventories and a physical capacitor. An exact bulk–shape decomposition, uniform fifteen-pair bulk comparison, and nonautonomous twelve-input kinetic response give a strict rational four-current feedback bound below 17/20, excluding nonstationary bounded complete attractor trajectories. Consequently the compact global attractor is the singleton {X*}, attracting each bounded admissible positive finite-second-moment preparation class in the stated weighted density/resource topology. The selected state retains positive stationary material circulation, chemical regeneration and energetic turnover. In the stationary large-population limit, interacting empirical-state laws converge weakly to the point mass at X*, with convergence of selected charge, current and caloric observables. Iterated population/time limits commute for the stated bounded continuous state observables but remain noncommuting for uninterrupted charge-band survival. These results concern the specified synthetic physical source; they do not imply calibrated biological function, a universal convergence rate, or indefinite finite-population path survival.

Current frontier

What the public record hands forward.

The published five-paper investigation now gives a connected account of mechanochemical movement, reciprocal resource and electrical feedback, interacting-population kinetics, unique maintained stationary organization, and global selection for the specified synthetic four-phase system. Paper V proves A = {X*} and qualitative class-uniform attraction for covered bounded positive finite-second-moment preparations, together with stationary empirical-population selection and convergence of selected physical observables. The time/population limits commute for specified bounded continuous state observables, whereas uninterrupted charge-band survival remains pathwise noncommuting. These theorems do not provide a universal numerical convergence rate, assert survival forever for finite populations, or establish a calibrated biological realization. Further work may investigate regenerative response, disturbance recovery, and transfer to other kinetic or biological systems.
