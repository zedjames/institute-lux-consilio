Kinetic Organization and Biological Function

How organized movement and coupled physical dynamics produce specified biological functions.

Guiding question

How does mechanochemical transport generate selected function through shared resources, and which kinetic information survives the passage from individual processes to collective dynamics?

Program

Research trajectory

Paper I establishes a reversible momentum-bearing mechanochemical transporter under prescribed chemical and ionic boundaries. Its hybrid physical generator identifies information lost under coarse phase/energy observation and gives certified function-specific transport and caloric results.

Paper II couples active transport, passive ionic return, chemical regeneration, finite shared inventories and membrane charge. A full kinetic invariant analysis distinguishes closed reversible Gibbs equilibrium from maintained nonequilibrium circulation with positive entropy production and zero mean net charge drift; its selected charge-band functionals quantify persistence, exit and recovery.

Paper III moves to populations of momentum-bearing transporters interacting through common ionic and chemical resources and an inventory-derived capacitor. Finite-population generators converge on stated positive finite windows to a nonlinear measure-valued kinetic–chemical–electrical law, with conditional propagation of chaos and selected fluctuation laws preserving physical shared-event covariance.

Collective prediction remains function-specific. Equal-energy population preparations with identical concentrations, phase fractions and voltage can produce different transport currents, and the actual loaded-rate generator has no invariant finite polynomial-momentum dictionary. A maintained Boltzmann-template projection retains an explicit discarded-kinetic residual, giving a reproducible strict finite-time charge-decrease certificate for a specified matched preparation. The series remains based on declared synthetic mechanisms; qìjī and qìhuà provide historical motivation without a claimed microscopic identification.

Paper IV examines the long-time organization of the same full kinetic source. Exact stationary reduction and rigorous source-specific interval enclosures identify one maintained stationary law with sustained ionic/chemical circulation, nontrivial phase–mechanical correlations and an in-band capacitor charge. It distinguishes closed Gibbs equilibrium from maintained organization, proves full-kinetic local attraction, and establishes a compact long-time attracting set supporting positive invariant-mean currents and recurring charge-band occupation. The finite-population indefinite-survival probability vanishes for each fixed population while the stationary collective limit stays in-band over every fixed horizon. The research retains the interpretive distinction between 氣機 (qìjī, organized movement) and 氣化 (qìhuà, material transformation) without claiming a calibrated biological realization.

Methods

  • Momentum-bearing hybrid Langevin–jump transport with guarded shared inventories
  • Eventwise conservation, electrical/capacitive work and pathwise energetic accounting
  • Invariant kinetic laws and nonequilibrium circulation for the one-transporter source
  • Nonlinear empirical-measure limits, kinetic martingales, conditional chaos and selected fluctuations
  • Observation-fiber nonclosure and residual-controlled finite-time collective predictions
  • Full kinetic stationary-response enclosures, semigroup stability, Hellinger dissipation, compact attractors and finite-population first-exit controls

From Kinetic Movement to Biological Function

Functional descent, historical energy, and certified observation in coupled transport processes

A synthetic reversible four-phase, single-ion mechanochemical transporter connects continuous mechanical motion to conformational jumps, chemical reservoirs, and signed ionic release. Equal observed phase and kinetic energy need not determine future function: at nonzero coupling two mechanical preparations have initial outside-release rates differing by a factor of approximately 1.35. Conditioning on transition histories yields a measure-valued predictive state. For specified finite windows, source-derived backward bounds certify signed transport and outside-edge event probability. Exact caloric path identities link endpoint phase, signed ion transport, and energy accounting. Results concern a specified synthetic source rather than a calibrated protein or a general physical definition of qi.

Coupled Kinetic Processes and the Maintenance of Biological Function

Shared finite reservoirs, sustained nonequilibrium circulation, and selected charge-band persistence

Extending Paper I from prescribed boundaries to dynamically shared resources, this study constructs a momentum-bearing four-phase ion transporter coupled to passive return, finite ionic and chemical inventories, and an inventory-derived membrane capacitor. Exact pathwise conservation and separate caloric/free-energy accounts support a complete hybrid generator with unique invariant kinetic laws. Closed chemistry gives reversible Gibbs equilibrium; maintained regeneration produces strictly positive pump–leak circulation and entropy production even with zero mean charge drift. Source-wide bounds distinguish stationary charge-band occupancy, exit flux, uninterrupted survival and extreme-charge recovery. These results describe a synthetic, explicitly normalized source rather than a calibrated physiological homeostasis threshold.

From Individual Transport to Collective Kinetic Function

Nonlinear population limits, kinetic observability, and collective functional prediction

A synthetic population of momentum-bearing transporters interacts through shared finite ionic and chemical reservoirs and an inventory-derived membrane capacitor. The exact diffusion–jump source yields a well-posed nonlinear kinetic–chemical–electrical population law, finite-time convergence under specified positive-resource preparations, conditional propagation of chaos and selected Gaussian fluctuations with shared-event covariations. Explicit equal-energy preparations demonstrate distinct currents despite matching concentrations, phase fractions and energy, and the loaded-current observable has no invariant finite polynomial-momentum generator dictionary. A residual-controlled maintained-source projection certifies 1.8932060449 < χ(0.05) < 1.9954528308 < χ(0) = 2 for a prescribed matched preparation; thermodynamic, entropy and charge-band results retain their declared conditions. These results are finite-time and synthetic, not calibrated claims about a biological population.

Stationary Organization and Functional Persistence in Collective Kinetic Systems

Full kinetic stationary classification, local stability, and persistent physical function

Paper IV studies stationary organization and long-time function in the synthetic collective kinetic–chemical–electrical transporter source of Papers I–III. The complete position–momentum–phase kinetic response gives exactly one maintained positive stationary organization with ongoing pump, passive-return and regeneration circulation, charge within the prescribed [1, 3] band, phase–position correlation, and non-Gaussian momentum structure. The full kinetic–resource linearization and nonlinear estimates establish an explicit local attracting neighborhood, while a separate compact attracting set supports strictly positive invariant-mean circulation and quantitative long-time charge-band recurrence. Native finite-population event paths eventually exit the band with probability one for each fixed population, whereas the stationary collective limit remains in-band over every fixed finite horizon; the resulting population/time survival limits do not commute. The work does not establish global point selection or identify the compact attractor with the stationary state; these remain for Paper V.

Current frontier

What the public record hands forward.

Papers I–IV now advance from individual mechanochemical motion through collective finite-resource transport to source-specific stationary organization and persistent physical function. Paper IV establishes one maintained positive stationary state, an explicit local attracting basin, a compact long-time attractor, invariant-mean circulation and charge-band recurrence, while distinguishing finite-population exit from collective stationary survival. It does not classify every maintained complete trajectory or prove that the global compact attractor is a singleton. Global point selection is the separate question developed in Paper V; calibration to measured biological mechanisms and observation protocols also remains open.

Open questions

  3. 03
