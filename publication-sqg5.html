A Classical Batalin–Vilkovisky Master Action for a Vacuum-Relative Einstein–Scalar Sector

Palatini gauge symmetry, local-functional closure, and Fock compatibility

Zed James · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23404955 ↗

Scholarly record

Research summary

Paper V constructs an intrinsic classical Batalin–Vilkovisky master action for Einstein gravity coupled to a real scalar with independent Palatini connection, diffeomorphism and projective gauge symmetries. The symmetric-projective local-functional master satisfies the classical master equation and generates a square-zero differential on the specified polynomial jet algebra, including antifield-dependent functionals. On Paper IV's nonlinear spherical branch, reduction identifies the same Fock Hamiltonian, closed operator domain, strong unitary evolution, boundary charge, and vacuum-relative source. The construction proves compatibility of the classical gauge law with the specified quantum observables; BV properness, a Fock-space BRST operator, and reference-independent renormalized stress are not established.
