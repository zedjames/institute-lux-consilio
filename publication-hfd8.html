Scientific abstract and context

Constitutive renewal and prospective capacity

Paper VIII extends a regenerative mechanochemical transporter by local storage for transformation-generated material. Matched intact preparations retain equal immediate transport rates yet exhibit strictly graded recovery probabilities after a common damage challenge on a proved short-time interval. Exact material balance, quadratic renewal-resistance coefficients and an access-disabled control distinguish residual material from Corruption for the specified demand. The model supplies a requirement-relative Health classification, controlled recovery-probability evaluation and finite-sample identification guarantees.

For retained occupancy b, the leading short-time renewal loss relative to the matched clean preparation is 3bλ(q₀)/8 times the squared horizon. With assembly-access feedback disabled, the projected carrier law is unchanged despite residue retention. The results concern the idealized regenerative source, with empirical measurement requiring its own calibration.
